# Dataset card: Weather Intelligence Assistant

The assistant's historical weather data covers **8 Indian cities**: Chennai, Delhi, Guwahati, Jaipur, Kolkata, Mumbai, Pune, Shimla.
It contains one row per city per day from **2015-01-01 to 2025-01-01**. There is **no data after 2025-01-01**:
questions about later dates cannot be answered from this dataset and need a live forecast or a newer data source.

Normal values in this knowledge base are **10-year averages (2015–2024)**, not the
30-year normals that the India Meteorological Department (IMD) uses, so anomalies here may differ from IMD bulletins.

## Variables

| Column | Meaning | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| temp_max | Daily maximum air temperature at 2 m | °C |
| temp_min | Daily minimum air temperature at 2 m | °C |
| precip | Daily total precipitation (rain + snow water equivalent) | mm |
| wind_max | Daily maximum sustained wind speed at 10 m (not gusts) | km/h |
| humidity | Daily mean relative humidity at 2 m | % |
| pressure | Daily mean sea-level pressure | hPa |
| weathercode | Most significant WMO weather code of the day | code (see WMO weather codes document) |

## Limitations

- The data comes from the Open-Meteo Historical Weather API. It is **reanalysis-based gridded data**, not readings
  from a single weather station, so local extremes (for example one neighbourhood's rainfall) can be underestimated.
- Days are aggregated in **UTC (GMT)**, not Indian Standard Time; a "day" runs from 05:30 to 05:30 IST.
- Wind is sustained wind at 10 m height. **Gusts and wind at height (e.g. at crane level) are higher** and are not in the data.
- Rainfall is a daily total. **Hourly rainfall intensity and flood water depth are not available.**
- The weather codes in this dataset never include thunderstorm (95–99) or fog (45, 48) codes, so thunderstorms
  and fog cannot be identified from it.
