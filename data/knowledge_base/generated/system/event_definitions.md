# Event definitions used in this knowledge base

All weather events (heatwaves, cold waves, heavy rain, dry spells, high wind, snowfall) in the event documents are
detected automatically from the daily data with the rules below. Thresholds follow India Meteorological Department
(IMD) criteria where IMD defines them. "Departure" means the difference from the normal for that calendar date,
where the normal is a 15-day smoothed average over 2015–2024.

These are **estimates from gridded data for a single location**. Official IMD declarations require the criteria at
several stations in a meteorological subdivision, so an official heatwave may be missing here, or the reverse.

## City zones

| Zone | Cities |
| --- | --- |
| plains | Delhi, Guwahati, Jaipur, Kolkata, Pune |
| coastal | Chennai, Mumbai |
| hills | Shimla |

## Heatwave (at least 2 consecutive days)

- **Plains:** max temperature ≥ 40°C and departure ≥ +4.5°C, or max temperature ≥ 45°C.
  Severe: max ≥ 40°C and departure ≥ +6.5°C, or max ≥ 47°C.
- **Coastal:** max temperature ≥ 37°C and departure ≥ +4.5°C. Severe: departure ≥ +6.5°C.
- **Hills:** max temperature ≥ 30°C and departure ≥ +4.5°C. Severe: departure ≥ +6.5°C.

## Cold wave (at least 2 consecutive days)

- **Plains:** min temperature ≤ 10°C and departure ≤ −4.5°C, or min temperature ≤ 4°C.
  Severe: min ≤ 10°C and departure ≤ −6.5°C, or min ≤ 2°C.
- **Coastal:** min temperature ≤ 15°C and departure ≤ −4.5°C. Severe: departure ≤ −6.5°C.
- **Hills:** min temperature ≤ 0°C and departure ≤ −4.5°C. Severe: departure ≤ −6.5°C.

## Rain

- **Rainy day:** at least 2.5 mm of rain.
- **Heavy rain:** 64.5–115.5 mm in a day. **Very heavy:** 115.6–204.4 mm. **Extremely heavy:** ≥ 204.5 mm.
  Consecutive heavy-rain days are grouped into one event.
- **Dry spell:** 10 or more consecutive days below 2.5 mm inside the city's main rainy season
  (June–September for all cities except Chennai, which uses October–December, its northeast monsoon season).

## Wind and snow

- **High wind:** daily maximum sustained wind ≥ 40 km/h. Gusts are not in the data and will be stronger.
- **Snowfall:** a day with weather code 71–77 (snow).

## Rainy-season onset estimate

The first date on or after May 20 (October 10 for Chennai) from which the next 5 days total at least 30 mm,
with at least 3 of those days being rainy days. This is **a rainfall-based estimate, not the official IMD
monsoon onset date**, and pre-monsoon showers can make it early.
