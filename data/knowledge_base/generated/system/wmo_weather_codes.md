# WMO weather codes used in the dataset

The `weathercode` column uses World Meteorological Organization (WMO) weather interpretation codes.
For daily data the code is the most significant weather of that day. Codes in the 50s mean drizzle,
60s mean rain, 70s mean snow, 80s mean showers and 95–99 mean thunderstorms.
