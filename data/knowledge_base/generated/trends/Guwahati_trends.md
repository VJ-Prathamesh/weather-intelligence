# Guwahati year-over-year trends (2015–2024)

Over 2015–2024, a straight-line fit shows an increase of about 0.9 °C per decade in the average daily maximum
temperature in Guwahati, a decrease of about 0.6 °C per decade in the average daily minimum, and
a decrease of about 22% per decade
in annual rainfall. The warmest year was 2023 (average maximum 29.1°C)
and the coolest was 2019 (27.9°C).

**Caution:** 10 years is too short to separate a climate trend from natural year-to-year variability
(for example El Niño and La Niña years). Treat these figures as a description of this decade, not a long-term trend.
