# Chennai rainy-season onset and seasonal rainfall

Chennai's main rainy season is the **northeast monsoon (October–December)**. The table gives, for each year, an estimated onset date of
sustained rain and the total rainfall of the season. The 2015–2024 average seasonal rainfall is
718 mm. The wettest season was 2021 (1082 mm) and the driest
was 2016 (288 mm).

The onset date is a **rainfall-based estimate, not the official IMD monsoon onset date** (see the event definitions document).
