# Guwahati rainy-season onset and seasonal rainfall

Guwahati's main rainy season is the **southwest monsoon (June–September)**. The table gives, for each year, an estimated onset date of
sustained rain and the total rainfall of the season. The 2015–2024 average seasonal rainfall is
1750 mm. The wettest season was 2016 (2261 mm) and the driest
was 2019 (1318 mm).

The onset date is a **rainfall-based estimate, not the official IMD monsoon onset date** (see the event definitions document).
