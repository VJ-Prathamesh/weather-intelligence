# Delhi rainy-season onset and seasonal rainfall

Delhi's main rainy season is the **southwest monsoon (June–September)**. The table gives, for each year, an estimated onset date of
sustained rain and the total rainfall of the season. The 2015–2024 average seasonal rainfall is
597 mm. The wettest season was 2024 (990 mm) and the driest
was 2020 (372 mm).

The onset date is a **rainfall-based estimate, not the official IMD monsoon onset date** (see the event definitions document).
