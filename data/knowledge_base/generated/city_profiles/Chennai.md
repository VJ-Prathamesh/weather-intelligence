# City climate profile: Chennai

Chennai (Tamil Nadu) is a coastal city on the Bay of Bengal, at latitude 13.11°N, longitude 80.25°E
and an elevation of about 10 m. For heatwave and cold-wave rules it is treated as a **coastal** location.

Based on 2015–2024 data, the hottest month in Chennai is **June**
(average maximum 35.2°C) and the coldest month is **January**
(average minimum 21.8°C). Normal annual rainfall is about **1311 mm**.
Most of its rain falls in the northeast monsoon (October–December); the wettest month is November (357 mm on average) and the driest
is February (5 mm). Months that each bring at least 10% of the annual rain:
August, September, October, November, December.
