# City climate profile: Shimla

Shimla (Himachal Pradesh) is a hill station in the western Himalaya, at latitude 31.11°N, longitude 77.17°E
and an elevation of about 1940 m. For heatwave and cold-wave rules it is treated as a **hills** location.

Based on 2015–2024 data, the hottest month in Shimla is **June**
(average maximum 24.5°C) and the coldest month is **January**
(average minimum -1.6°C). Normal annual rainfall is about **1359 mm**.
Most of its rain falls in the southwest monsoon (June–September); the wettest month is July (322 mm on average) and the driest
is November (24 mm). Months that each bring at least 10% of the annual rain:
July, August, September.
