# City climate profile: Mumbai

Mumbai (Maharashtra) is a coastal city on the Arabian Sea, at latitude 19.09°N, longitude 72.85°E
and an elevation of about 6 m. For heatwave and cold-wave rules it is treated as a **coastal** location.

Based on 2015–2024 data, the hottest month in Mumbai is **May**
(average maximum 32.9°C) and the coldest month is **January**
(average minimum 18.5°C). Normal annual rainfall is about **2266 mm**.
Most of its rain falls in the southwest monsoon (June–September); the wettest month is July (781 mm on average) and the driest
is April (0 mm). Months that each bring at least 10% of the annual rain:
June, July, August, September.
