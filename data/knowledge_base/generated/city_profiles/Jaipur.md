# City climate profile: Jaipur

Jaipur (Rajasthan) is a semi-arid inland city on the eastern edge of the Thar desert region, at latitude 26.89°N, longitude 75.83°E
and an elevation of about 430 m. For heatwave and cold-wave rules it is treated as a **plains** location.

Based on 2015–2024 data, the hottest month in Jaipur is **May**
(average maximum 39.9°C) and the coldest month is **January**
(average minimum 8.4°C). Normal annual rainfall is about **624 mm**.
Most of its rain falls in the southwest monsoon (June–September); the wettest month is July (218 mm on average) and the driest
is February (4 mm). Months that each bring at least 10% of the annual rain:
June, July, August, September.
