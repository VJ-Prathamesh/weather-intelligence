# City climate profile: Guwahati

Guwahati (Assam) is a city in the Brahmaputra valley, northeast India, at latitude 26.12°N, longitude 91.77°E
and an elevation of about 52 m. For heatwave and cold-wave rules it is treated as a **plains** location.

Based on 2015–2024 data, the hottest month in Guwahati is **August**
(average maximum 31.2°C) and the coldest month is **January**
(average minimum 11.8°C). Normal annual rainfall is about **2502 mm**.
Most of its rain falls in the southwest monsoon (June–September); the wettest month is July (547 mm on average) and the driest
is November (12 mm). Months that each bring at least 10% of the annual rain:
May, June, July, August, September.
