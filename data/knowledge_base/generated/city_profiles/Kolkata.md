# City climate profile: Kolkata

Kolkata (West Bengal) is a city on the Hooghly river in the Gangetic delta, about 100 km inland from the Bay of Bengal, at latitude 22.60°N, longitude 88.32°E
and an elevation of about 12 m. For heatwave and cold-wave rules it is treated as a **plains** location.

Based on 2015–2024 data, the hottest month in Kolkata is **April**
(average maximum 35.6°C) and the coldest month is **January**
(average minimum 13.3°C). Normal annual rainfall is about **1882 mm**.
Most of its rain falls in the southwest monsoon (June–September); the wettest month is July (402 mm on average) and the driest
is January (16 mm). Months that each bring at least 10% of the annual rain:
June, July, August, September.
