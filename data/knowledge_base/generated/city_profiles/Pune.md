# City climate profile: Pune

Pune (Maharashtra) is a city on the Deccan plateau, on the leeward side of the Western Ghats, at latitude 18.52°N, longitude 73.87°E
and an elevation of about 542 m. For heatwave and cold-wave rules it is treated as a **plains** location.

Based on 2015–2024 data, the hottest month in Pune is **April**
(average maximum 37.4°C) and the coldest month is **January**
(average minimum 15.1°C). Normal annual rainfall is about **1096 mm**.
Most of its rain falls in the southwest monsoon (June–September); the wettest month is July (281 mm on average) and the driest
is January (3 mm). Months that each bring at least 10% of the annual rain:
June, July, August, September.
